Knowledge Base

NEED TO KNOW

DO I NEED EXPERIENCE TO JOIN?

Absolutely not. 80% of our members started with zero experience. Our Fundamentals classes are designed specifically for Day 1 beginners to learn proper technique safely without the pressure of sparring.

WHAT GEAR DO I NEED FOR DAY 1?

Just bring comfortable gym clothes, a water bottle, and a towel. We have rental gloves available for your first few sessions. Once you commit, we recommend buying your own hand wraps and gloves (available at our front desk).

DO YOU HAVE SHOWERS & PARKING?

Yes. We have fully equipped locker rooms with hot showers. We also have a dedicated parking lot for members directly in front of the gym (free for 2 hours with validation).

CAN I FREEZE MY MEMBERSHIP?

Yes. For injuries or travel longer than 2 weeks, you can freeze your membership for a small administrative fee. Please notify the front desk at least 5 days in advance via email.
